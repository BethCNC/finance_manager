# Finance Manager User Guide

## Welcome to Finance Manager

Finance Manager is a comprehensive personal finance application designed to help you track expenses, manage budgets, set financial goals, and get AI-powered insights into your spending habits.

## Getting Started

### 1. First Time Setup

**Accessing the App**:
- Open your web browser and navigate to the Finance Manager URL
- The app works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- No installation required - it's a Progressive Web App (PWA)

**Initial Configuration**:
1. **Connect Your Bank Accounts**: Use Plaid integration to securely connect your bank accounts
2. **Set Up Categories**: Customize expense categories to match your spending patterns
3. **Create Your First Budget**: Set monthly spending limits for different categories
4. **Define Financial Goals**: Set short-term and long-term financial objectives

### 2. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of your financial health:

**Summary Cards**:
- **Total Income**: Your monthly income from all sources
- **Total Expenses**: Your monthly spending across all categories
- **Net Position**: Income minus expenses (positive = savings, negative = overspending)
- **Budget Status**: How you're performing against your budget

**Quick Actions**:
- **Add Transaction**: Quickly record a new expense or income
- **View Reports**: Access detailed financial reports
- **AI Advisor**: Get personalized financial advice
- **Account Sync**: Refresh data from connected accounts

## Core Features

### 1. Transaction Management

**Adding Transactions**:
1. Click the "+" button on the transactions page
2. Fill in the transaction details:
   - **Description**: What you spent money on
   - **Amount**: The dollar amount (negative for expenses, positive for income)
   - **Date**: When the transaction occurred
   - **Category**: Select from predefined categories
   - **Account**: Which account the transaction came from
   - **Person**: Who made the transaction (Beth, Bryan, or Joint)
   - **Business**: Check if it's a business expense
   - **Subscription**: Check if it's a recurring subscription
   - **Notes**: Additional details

**Editing Transactions**:
1. Find the transaction in your list
2. Click the three dots menu (⋮) next to the transaction
3. Select "Edit" from the dropdown
4. Make your changes and save

**Deleting Transactions**:
1. Click the three dots menu (⋮) next to the transaction
2. Select "Delete" from the dropdown
3. Confirm the deletion

**Bulk Operations**:
1. Click the grid icon in the header to enable bulk mode
2. Select multiple transactions using checkboxes
3. Use bulk actions to:
   - Delete multiple transactions
   - Categorize multiple transactions
   - Export selected transactions

### 2. Budget Management

**Creating a Budget**:
1. Navigate to the Budget page
2. Click "Create Budget" or "Edit Budget"
3. Set spending limits for each category:
   - **Food & Groceries**: Dining out, groceries, food delivery
   - **Transportation**: Gas, public transit, rideshare
   - **Entertainment**: Movies, streaming services, hobbies
   - **Home & Utilities**: Rent, utilities, maintenance
   - **Healthcare**: Medical expenses, insurance
   - **Personal Care**: Clothing, grooming, personal items
   - **Shopping**: General merchandise, electronics
   - **Education**: Courses, books, training
   - **Travel**: Vacations, business travel
   - **Insurance**: Life, health, auto insurance
   - **Taxes**: Income tax, property tax
   - **Investments**: Retirement contributions, investments
   - **Savings**: Emergency fund, general savings
   - **Debt Payment**: Credit cards, loans, mortgages
   - **Business Expenses**: Work-related costs
   - **Software & Subscriptions**: Apps, software licenses
   - **Family**: Childcare, family expenses
   - **Transfer Fee**: Bank transfer fees
   - **Gifts**: Presents, donations
   - **Pet Care**: Veterinary, pet supplies

**Budget Tracking**:
- **Green**: Under budget (spending less than allocated)
- **Yellow**: Close to budget limit (80-100% of allocation)
- **Red**: Over budget (spending more than allocated)

**AI Budget Optimization**:
- Get personalized recommendations for budget adjustments
- Identify areas where you can save money
- Receive suggestions for next month's budget allocation

### 3. Financial Goals

**Setting Goals**:
1. Navigate to the Goals page
2. Click "Add Goal"
3. Define your goal:
   - **Name**: Descriptive name for your goal
   - **Target Amount**: How much you want to save
   - **Current Amount**: How much you've saved so far
   - **Target Date**: When you want to achieve the goal
   - **Category**: Type of goal (Emergency Fund, Vacation, etc.)
   - **Priority**: High, Medium, or Low
   - **Description**: Additional details

**Goal Types**:
- **Emergency Fund**: 3-6 months of expenses
- **Vacation**: Travel and leisure expenses
- **Home Purchase**: Down payment for a house
- **Car Purchase**: New or used vehicle
- **Education**: College or training expenses
- **Retirement**: Long-term retirement savings
- **Debt Payoff**: Credit card or loan payments
- **Investment**: Stock market or other investments

**Tracking Progress**:
- Visual progress bars show completion percentage
- Milestone celebrations when you reach 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%
- Automatic progress updates based on your savings

## Troubleshooting

### 1. Common Issues

**Transaction Not Syncing**:
- Check internet connection
- Verify bank account connection
- Try manual refresh
- Contact support if issue persists

**Budget Not Updating**:
- Ensure transactions are properly categorized
- Check date ranges for budget calculations
- Verify budget amounts are set correctly
- Refresh the page

**AI Advisor Not Responding**:
- Check internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear browser cache
- Contact support if issue persists

### 2. Data Issues

**Missing Transactions**:
- Check date filters
- Verify account connections
- Look for uncategorized transactions
- Use search function

**Incorrect Categories**:
- Edit transaction categories manually
- Use bulk categorization for multiple transactions
- Set up automatic categorization rules
- Review category assignments regularly

**Budget Calculations**:
- Verify transaction dates
- Check category assignments
- Ensure budget amounts are correct
- Review spending patterns
